The finding first

Adjusted Rand index between the two halves of the period is 0.156. Below 0.5 — these clusters are noise, not a taxonomy. Reported as such rather than re-tuned until they look stable.

Out-of-sample Spearman IC is +0.0941 and R² is -0.0562. R2 of -0.056 is at or below zero, which is the expected outcome and not a failure.

Gradient boosting, read for diagnosis

purged CV over 5 folds (14 purged, 0 embargoed)
  Spearman IC +0.0941 | pinball q50=0.1822, q90=0.1748 | R2 -0.0562

The headline metric is not R². On a heavy-tailed target it is dominated by a handful of observations and swings across folds for reasons unrelated to learning (spec review A11). Spearman rank IC and pinball loss are the metrics; R² is shown because §6 expects to see it near zero, and it should be visible when it is.

14 training samples were purged for label-window overlap and 0 more embargoed. Removing either to "keep more data" would be refused: the discarded rows are precisely the contaminated ones.
